The content on this website is for general information and education about Ayurveda. It is not medical advice, and it does not replace a consultation with a qualified doctor.

No promise of a cure

Ayurvedic treatment at Vednath Ayurved is planned individually after an assessment. Results vary from person to person. Nothing on this website promises or guarantees a cure for any disease or condition.

Keep your other treatment

Please don’t stop or change any medicine or treatment prescribed by another doctor without discussing it with that doctor. Where Ayurvedic care is offered for serious illnesses such as cancer, it is supportive and complementary, alongside your specialist’s treatment.

Emergencies

This website and the booking form are not for emergencies. In a medical emergency, call 112 or go to your nearest hospital.

Self-check tools

The Prakriti quiz is a general awareness tool. It is not a diagnosis. Your actual Prakriti and any imbalance are assessed by the doctor through Nadi Pariksha and a full consultation.

Patient reviews

Reviews describe individual experiences. They are not a guarantee of similar results.

Articles

Blog articles share general, traditional Ayurvedic guidance. Your own diet and routine should be based on your Prakriti, your health and your doctor’s advice.
