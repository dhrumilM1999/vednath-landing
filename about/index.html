About Vednath Ayurved

Your journey to wellness, rooted in Ayurveda.

An Ayurveda & Panchakarma hospital in Adajan, Surat, offering natural Ayurvedic treatment to people of all ages, in person and online.

The cause, not only the symptom.

At Vednath Ayurved, treatment begins by understanding why a problem started, not only what it feels like today.

Every plan follows classical Ayurvedic texts and traditional practice. It starts with Nadi Pariksha (pulse diagnosis) and Prakriti assessment (your body constitution), and leads to a personal combination of herbal medicine, diet, daily and seasonal routines (Dinacharya and Ritucharya) and, where it is indicated, Panchakarma.

Care is available at the clinic in Adajan and through online consultations, so patients outside Surat can begin their treatment from home.
