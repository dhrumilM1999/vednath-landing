Our approach

Ayurveda for the cause, not only the symptom.

Every plan at Vednath Ayurved follows classical texts and traditional practice, and starts by understanding you: your pulse, your constitution and your daily life.

  1. Book by call or WhatsApp

    Visit the clinic in Adajan, or start with an online consultation from anywhere.

  2. Nadi & Prakriti assessment

    Pulse diagnosis (Nadi Pariksha) and an analysis of your body constitution (Prakriti).

  3. Your personalised plan

    Herbal medicine, a diet chart and daily and seasonal routines (Dinacharya, Ritucharya).

  4. Panchakarma, if needed

    Recommended only when your assessment shows it will help.

  5. Follow-ups

    In person or online, adjusting the plan as you progress.

The Panchakarma journey

Three stages, one reset.

  1. Stage 01

    Purvakarma Preparation

    The body is readied for cleansing, traditionally through oleation (Snehana) and therapeutic sweating (Swedana).

    • Snehana
    • Swedana

  2. Stage 02

    Pradhan Karma The main therapy

    One or more of the five classical karmas, chosen for you after assessment.

    • Vamana
    • Virechana
    • Basti
    • Nasya
    • Raktamokshana

  3. Stage 03

    Paschat Karma After-care

    A gradual return to normal food and routine (Samsarjana Krama), with diet and lifestyle guidance to help the benefits last.

    • Samsarjana Krama
    • Diet & routine
