01How we workઅમારી પદ્ધતિ

The cause first. Then the plan.

  1. Book by call or WhatsApp

    કૉલ અથવા WhatsApp પર બુક કરો

    Come to the clinic in Adajan, or start with an online consultation from anywhere.

  2. Nadi & Prakriti assessment

    નાડી અને પ્રકૃતિ પરીક્ષણ

    The doctor reads your pulse and your body constitution: Vata, Pitta, Kapha.

  3. A plan written for you

    તમારા માટેનો પ્લાન

    Herbal medicine, a diet chart, and daily and seasonal routines (Dinacharya, Ritucharya).

  4. Panchakarma, only if needed

    જરૂર હોય તો જ પંચકર્મ

    Recommended when your assessment shows it will help, never as a default package.

  5. Follow-ups

    ફૉલો-અપ

    In person or online, adjusting the plan as you go.
