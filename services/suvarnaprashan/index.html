Suvarnaprashan

A traditional Ayurvedic sanskar offered for children’s growth, development and immunity.

A golden tradition

Suvarnaprashan Sanskar is one of the traditional sanskars of Ayurveda for children.

At Vednath Ayurved it is offered for children’s development and for the common health problems of childhood, alongside regular paediatric care.

Traditionally, Suvarnaprashan is given on Pushya Nakshatra days.

Suvarnaprashan is a traditional Ayurvedic practice. It doesn’t replace your child’s routine vaccinations or paediatric check-ups.
