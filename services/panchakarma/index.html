Specialised therapies

Local therapies

Warm medicated oils, ghee and herbal powders, focused on one part of the body.

Neck

Greeva Basti

Warm medicated oil held over the neck in a dough ring. Used for cervical pain, stiffness and spondylosis.

Knees

Janu Basti

Warm oil held over the knees. Used for osteoarthritis and knee degeneration.

Lower back

Kati Basti

Warm oil pooled over the lower back. Used for sciatica, lumbar spondylosis and disc problems.

Eyes

Netra Tarpan

Ghee eye therapy for dryness and eye fatigue.

Whole body

Udvartana

Herbal powder massage for exfoliation, circulation and fat reduction.

The journey

Three stages, one reset

01

Purvakarma

Preparation, traditionally through oleation (Snehana) and therapeutic sweating (Swedana).

02

Pradhan Karma

The main therapy: one or more of the five karmas, chosen after assessment.

03

Paschat Karma

After-care: a gradual return to normal food and routine (Samsarjana Krama).
