Garbh Sanskar

Ayurvedic Garbh Sanskar for the physical, mental and intellectual development of the baby in the womb.

An ancient practice for mother and child

Garbh Sanskar is a traditional Ayurvedic practice for the mother and the unborn child, using diet, thoughts and environment.

At Vednath Ayurved, care can begin before conception with Garbhadhan Sanskar, Ayurvedic preparation for healthy progeny, and continue through pregnancy with Garbh Sanskar.

What the tradition focuses on

  • Nurturing the baby’s qualities through the mother’s diet, thoughts and environment
  • Balancing the doshas and preparing the body
  • Supporting the baby’s brain and personality development from early life
  • Positive values and emotional strength

Garbh Sanskar complements, and never replaces, regular check-ups with your obstetrician.
