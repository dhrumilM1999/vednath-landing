Consultation, read through the pulse.

An in-depth assessment by an Ayurvedic doctor, and a plan made only for you.

What happens in a consultation

  1. 01

    In-depth health assessment

    The doctor listens to your history, symptoms, routine and diet.

  2. 02

    Nadi Pariksha & Prakriti analysis

    Pulse diagnosis and an assessment of your body constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha).

  3. 03

    Your customised plan

    Diet, lifestyle changes and herbal support, explained step by step.

  4. 04

    Panchakarma advice, if recommended

    Only when your assessment shows it will help.

Included

  • Ayurvedic remedies
  • Lifestyle guidance
  • A customised diet chart
