The booking form

When you use the booking form, your details are not sent to or stored on this website. The form simply prepares a message and opens WhatsApp on your device. Nothing is shared until you press send in WhatsApp yourself. From then on, the message is handled by WhatsApp (Meta) under its own privacy policy, and by the clinic as part of your appointment.

We ask only for what is needed to arrange an appointment: your name, mobile number, preferred date, consultation type and, if you wish, a short description of your concern. Please don’t send detailed medical reports through the form.

The Prakriti quiz

Your quiz answers are processed entirely in your browser. They are not sent anywhere and are gone when you leave the page.
